I'm **teo**.  
I build things. Software, hardware, and more and more of both at once.  
Catholic and e/acc, and no, that's not a contradiction.

This codex is where I think out loud.  
Programming, engineering, robotics, defense, philosophy, entrepreneurship. Whatever I can't stop thinking about ends up here, and the parts that survive the thinking get published.

My bias is simple: accelerate.  
Technology is how we take care of the Garden, and building it is a vocation, not a hobby. We were made to capture more energy, climb higher and build further than any generation before us.  
But capability without wisdom is just a faster way to fall. Every tool we ever made got turned against us the moment we forgot what it was for.  
So build harder, and pray harder. Acceleration ordered toward VIRTUE.  
That tension is most of what I write about.

The most ambitious thing I've built so far is [Orabimus](https://orabimus.com), a place to pray the Holy Rosary online.  
Ambitious not only on the technical side, where I pushed it to SOTA in lightweightness and performance, a web app that actually runs faster than native apps, but mostly for what it is: an extreme long-term goal of helping faithful Catholics (and why not other Christians?) pray together through the Rosary, feel less alone, more hopeful, and grow closer to God.  
English or Latin, alone or live with people from all over the world in the same room, everyone carrying each other's intentions.  
Free & ad-free. No investors, no sign-in.  
It grew out of the open Rosaries I've led on X since early 2024.  
Come pray with us. [@orabimus](https://x.com/orabimus)
