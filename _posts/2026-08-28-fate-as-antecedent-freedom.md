## Overture: The Whole Machine {#overture-the-whole-machine}

The familiar argument between free will and determinism usually begins too late. It begins with a person already inside the world, already embodied, already formed by a family and a language, already downstream of genetics and institutions and strangers. At some moment that person deliberates, and philosophy asks whether the decision can be free if the state of the world already fixes what comes next.

This model moves the decisive act of sourcehood upstream.

The wager is that a life can express agency without that agency interrupting physical history at every moment. Persons may instead contribute, antecedently to embodied history, a limited set of existential parameters. They are not scenes or biographies but ranges of possible structure: kinds of exposure, relation, vocation, loss, and authority, among other recurrent forms. Because lives intersect, no one can hold a private fate. Every antecedent contribution has to be reconciled with every other into one mutually coherent world before that world is physically instantiated. History then runs as one actual causal order, experienced from inside as deliberation, effort, and consequence.

The sequence can be stated before any part of it is defended:

> [antecedent agency](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom/#relocating-the-choice) → [limited selection](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom/#the-limited-menu) → [providential integration](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom/#integration-into-one-world) → [physical initialization](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom/#physical-execution) → [irreversible temporal execution](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom/#the-physical-horizon)

Thermodynamics supplies the direction of that execution: the world begins rich in exploitable differences and proceeds through processes that dissipate them. Astronomy supplies a shared clock, since the state of the sky at any moment is calculable in advance. Astrology is a separate and defeasible extension: the proposal that historical celestial grammar carries a reproducible semantics for qualitative phases of the same world-history. Archetypal language, where it helps, describes the recurrent class of manifestation rather than a literal scene. If the astrological semantics fails, the theory loses its proposed semantic decoder and keeps antecedent agency, global integration, and the thermodynamic arrow.

Three prohibitions hold from the start. No scene-selection: the model does not say a person chose each event that later occurred. No private fate: a biography is a local projection of one world that also contains other agents, natural laws, inherited conditions, and relational collisions. No conversion of suffering into consent: an event can belong to a person's life without having been selected by that person, and another agent's wrongdoing stays that agent's wrongdoing.

Four kinds of claim appear below and stay distinct. A physical anchor comes from contemporary science. A historical witness is something a text or tradition actually teaches. A structural analogy resembles part of the model without matching it. A proposed synthesis is machinery introduced here. Plato, Genesis, Qur'an 7:172, Aquinas, Plotinus, Jung, thermodynamics, and Buddhist cosmology do not secretly teach one forgotten doctrine. They constrain, resist, or anticipate fragments of a new architecture. Source defenses and boundaries live in the companion *References*.

## Before Difference {#before-difference}

*Before difference* is a conceptual boundary, not necessarily a claim about every conceivable form of succession. The person is considered before the physical run has differentiated identity through a particular body, family, language, career, loss, and historical sequence.

Several traditions offer compact precedents for this possibility. Genesis gives a root act whose consequences exceed the moment of choice. Qur'an 7:172 gives a primordial human-divine affirmation connected to later accountability without ordinary autobiographical recollection. Plato's *Myth of Er* comes closer to the mechanism itself: souls encounter patterns of lives, choose, become bound within Necessity, forget, and return to birth. None of these is the theory. Together they show that identity, responsibility, choice, and ordinary memory have not always been assumed to occupy the same temporal location. (See: [Adamic root agency](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#adamic-root-agency); [Covenant of Alast](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#covenant-of-alast); [Myth of Er](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#myth-of-er))

A narrower biblical motif adds one identity claim and no more. Passages of calling, prenatal vocation, and divine naming portray persons as particular to God before their full biography is available to them as autobiographical knowledge. Call this divine individuation. It is not premortal self-naming, and it does not solve numerical identity. (See: [Divine individuation](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#divine-individuation-called-by-name))

## Relocating the Choice {#relocating-the-choice}

The standard free-will problem makes freedom and determinism compete for the same causal joint. If the world-state at one moment plus the laws fixes the next state, where does an undetermined choice enter? Ordinary compatibilism answers by loosening what freedom requires: an action is free when it expresses the agent's own reasons, desires, and character, even if those states have causes.

This proposal moves differently. It grants something closer to libertarian sourcehood and relocates its strongest form to the conditions under which temporal history is realized. Deliberation still occurs. People weigh reasons, change their minds, persuade one another, and act, and those processes are among the causes through which the history happens rather than spectators watching a script. The claim is that the deepest authorship need not be recreated as an uncaused opening at every moment.

Compatibilism may be sufficient if freedom means acting without coercion from reasons, dispositions, and character that count as one's own. But this project asks for a stronger kind of sourcehood. Those reasons and dispositions may themselves be wholly received from a causal history the creature did not help constitute: heredity, environment, prior events, or, within classical theism, the providentially actualized order. Compatibilism then secures ownership of the action without securing creaturely authorship of the source from which the action flows.

Antecedent agency exists to pay that price. It is needed if the creature is to contribute to the configuration from which temporal character and action later emerge. Compatibilism can secure **free execution**. This theory asks whether there can also be **creaturely authorship of the executable**. (See: [Research §1.4](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s1-4))

Relocation alone, however, does not solve sourcehood. If the antecedent choice merely expresses an already fixed prior character, the original problem has been moved backward rather than answered. If it is not sufficiently fixed by a prior character, the opposite objection appears: what distinguishes genuine origination from arbitrariness or luck? The theory therefore inherits the libertarian first-act problem. It must eventually explain how an act can be intelligible through reasons and genuinely attributable to an agent without simply being the inevitable output of some still earlier dispositional state. Until that account is supplied, *antecedent sourcehood* names the place where the theory wants origination to occur; it does not yet explain how origination works. (See: [Origination regress / first-act problem](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#origination-regress-first-act-problem); [Research §1.5](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s1-5))

Composition and performance is the useful comparison. A score can originate in agency and then be fixed, and being fixed during performance does not mean it was never authored. That establishes one logical point: authorship and fixed execution stop contradicting one another once they are assigned to different explanatory stages.

That is why the model is better called antecedent sourcehood plus temporal determinacy than ordinary compatibilism. It keeps the intuition that the responsible subject genuinely contributes to what gets actualized, and it lets the physical run be settled at the global level once initialized.

## The Limited Menu {#the-limited-menu}

The naive version fails immediately: people cannot independently choose complete biographies.

Two candidates both select *I win the same election*, and both cannot be realized in one world. One person selects *I marry this individual* while the other selects *I never meet that person*. A parent selects an early death while a child independently selects a life in which parental loss never occurs. Complete scripts collide because lives are intersections rather than separable programs. The antecedent contribution therefore has to be less concrete than a biography and more constraining than a slogan.

Call it authorial parameterization. A subject selects or accepts a high-level existential parameter together with a sufficiently understood range of admissible realizations, while the concrete event stays unspecified:

> known parameter → known admissible range → unknown concrete realization

A parameter such as *separation* could appear as migration, bereavement, retirement, or renunciation. *Authority* could appear as parenthood, office, care, or conflict with a superior. *Creation* could appear as art, research, engineering, reproduction, or institution-building. The list illustrates the level; it is not canonical.

This middle level is hard to specify and impossible to skip. If any possible life counts as a parameter's realization, antecedent authorship is empty. If the parameter specifies who, when, and through which act, full script-selection returns. A valid parameter constrains the future enough to be attributable to the chooser while leaving the concrete realization open to integration.

That distinction blocks the most morally destructive reading of premortal choice. Selecting a risk envelope or a broad form of exposure is not selecting another person's later action. If person A harms person B, the act stays A's act. B's life can contain the consequence without B having authored the action. The model cannot infer consent to a harm from the fact that the harm shows up in the realized biography.

The phrase *you chose your life* is therefore too blunt to use. The narrower claim is that you may have authorially constrained the space from which your life could be realized.

The parameter vocabulary itself is an open research problem. A mature basis would have to be generative, non-redundant, recurrent across contexts, abstract enough to avoid scene-selection, and compressive enough to explain many concrete forms with few structural dimensions. Jung matters later because archetypal thinking treats very different events as instances of one recurrent form, but Jung does not hand over the menu. (See: [Limited archetypal selection](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#limited-archetypal-selection); [Archetype](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#archetype); [Parameter-basis problem](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#parameter-basis-problem); [Research §2](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s2))

Freedom here selects constraints rather than scenes.

## Integration into One World {#integration-into-one-world}

Once selection is limited rather than scene-level, the central problem changes. It is no longer *what did each person choose?* It is *how can all those contributions exist together?*

There is no private fate. One person's life is partly constituted by other people's choices, inherited institutions, physical regularities, biological relations, and historical accidents. A migration changes the lives of descendants not yet born. A scientific discovery changes the choices available to strangers. A war connects people who never meet. A natural disaster changes thousands of local histories at once. If antecedent agency exists, it has to be solved at the level of a world rather than a stack of personal scripts.

The model calls this providential integration. Compilation is one metaphor: many constrained contributions have to be made mutually compossible before a single world can be instantiated. No classical theologian described providence that way. Leibniz's compossibility is the closer philosophical analogy, since individually describable possibilities cannot simply be combined without regard for whether they can coexist in one complete world. (See: [Compossibility](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#compossibility); [Providential integration](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#providential-integration))

The realized world depends on a global reconciliation of every person's constrained contribution. What that reconciliation is remains open: what a domain of parameters looks like, what satisfies a constraint, whether the solution is unique. (See: [Schematic notation](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#schematic-notation); [Research §2.1](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s2-1))

Constraints differ in kind. Some could be hard, constitutive of the selected life-structure. Some could be soft, satisfiable through degrees or alternative realizations. Some are relational and impossible without another person. Some are permissions or risk envelopes rather than desired events. Some realizations may be morally or logically forbidden. All of them are constrained by the stable nomic structure of the physical world.

Typed constraints stop providence from turning into a utility maximizer that launders every outcome as collectively optimal. If one person's agency harms another, the theory cannot answer *the harmed person selected this*. It has to ask instead why this world permits the intersection, and what honoring the harmed person's antecedent parameters means when the event was not selected.

So the model relocates the problem of evil without solving it. A theory that preserved personal sourcehood by declaring every victim the secret author of every harm would be morally worse than the problem it set out to address.

A biography is a local projection of a world, and not every fact inside the biography belongs to the antecedent contribution of the local subject. Events can arise from other agents, from natural law, from inherited history, or from the integration itself. An event can belong to my life without having been authored by me. Whatever antecedent freedom turns out to be, it participates in one shared history.

## Who Chooses, and in What Mode {#who-chooses-and-in-what-mode}

The previous phases can be understood without deciding what an antecedent subject is. This one cannot.

The theory's deepest identity claim is simple and costly: the antecedent author has to be the embodied experiencer in the morally relevant sense. If some other being selected the parameters and I only suffer their execution, sourcehood has been outsourced rather than relocated.

Memory cannot carry the identity relation by itself. Ordinary persons forget vast parts of their lives without becoming numerically new subjects. Plato's Myth of Er and the Covenant of Alast are useful because they show traditions imagining responsibility or choice across radical epistemic discontinuity. Biblical divine individuation adds the thought that God's recognition of a person can exceed that person's present self-knowledge. None of this proves that a premortal chooser is identical with the embodied person. It makes the question intelligible rather than absurd. (See: [Personal identity](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#personal-identity); [Divine individuation](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#divine-individuation-called-by-name); [Myth of Er](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#myth-of-er); [Covenant of Alast](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#covenant-of-alast))

The strong version therefore needs a continuity relation more fundamental than episodic memory. Soul-continuity is the clearest candidate, holding that the same responsible subject exists across antecedent and embodied modes. That pays one debt by incurring another. It is not standard Thomistic hylomorphism. Aquinas treats the human being as a body-soul composite and gives no doctrine of fully constituted premortal persons selecting life parameters. The theory can borrow Aquinas on God, providence, and primary and secondary causation without pretending he supplies its anthropology. Its strong form is better described as a Christian-theistic speculative reconstruction with Platonic or premortal anthropological commitments. (See: [Hylomorphism](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#hylomorphism); [Primary and secondary causation](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#primary-and-secondary-causation))

A second boundary concerns what *antecedent* means. There are three serious options and no cost-free fourth.

Under premortal created succession, created subjects exist in an antecedent order with some form of succession and make their selections before our embodied physical history. This best preserves intuitive creaturely authorship. It also stands furthest from mainstream Christian anthropology and requires an account of what a premortal subject is.

Under atemporal creaturely agency, the created subject acts without succession. This preserves the strongest sense of *outside time* and carries the hardest metaphysical burden. Deliberation appears to involve comparison, ordering, and decision, and classical theism's attribution of eternity to God does not license finite creatures inheriting that mode of agency.

Under ontological antecedence without a premortal episode, the person's volitional identity is prior in the order of explanation to temporal manifestation, perhaps as comprehended within divine knowledge, with no narrated earlier life. This fits classical theism most easily and threatens the prize: if God alone selects which possible volitional profile becomes actual, the deepest authorship may have returned to God.

The trilemma should stay open until one horn is actually defended.

The same restraint clarifies Christian compatibility. The model fits well with classical theism's distinction between God and the created causal order. It has a real structural analogy with Adamic root agency. It can distinguish Christian eschatological consummation from physical cosmology. Its principal Christian cost is anthropology rather than God's causal relation to creation. (See: [Christian compatibility matrix](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#christian-compatibility-matrix))

This phase ends with two debts and no victory declaration: who is the antecedent author, and in what mode can that author act? If those debts cannot be paid, the core theory fails.

## Physical Execution {#physical-execution}

Assume for now that the boundary can be crossed, so that a responsible subject contributes antecedent constraints, those constraints are globally reconciled, and one compossible world is selected for realization. The theory now enters physics.

God should not be imagined as the first physical event. Aquinas helps here because primary and secondary causation operate at different explanatory levels. A star can form because a gas cloud undergoes gravitational collapse, and God can be called Creator, without the gravitational explanation becoming fake. Divine causality concerns the existence and ordering of created causal reality; physical causality describes transitions within it. Providential integration likewise does not need to be an extra force inserted between particles. (See: [Primary and secondary causation](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#primary-and-secondary-causation))

The physical world can then contain ordinary cosmic and biological development. Nothing in the model requires modern human bodies to appear instantaneously at initialization. Cosmic expansion, stellar nucleosynthesis, planetary formation, chemistry, Darwinian evolution, and human embodiment all belong inside the execution layer. Evolution is a mechanism through which the integrated world becomes historically concrete rather than a competitor to the metaphysics. (See: [Evolution and the execution layer](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#evolution-and-the-execution-layer))

Thermodynamics is the model's most important scientific anchor, and it gives execution a direction. A physical history is more than a sequence of labeled moments. Our macroscopic world shows a persistent arrow: heat spreads under ordinary conditions, systems approach equilibrium, organisms maintain local organization by consuming usable gradients and exporting entropy, and memories point toward what we call the past. Microscopic laws and the foundations of the arrow raise deep problems, but macroscopic irreversibility and nonequilibrium thermodynamics are far firmer than any astrological claim here. (See: [Thermodynamic arrow](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#thermodynamic-arrow))

The popular idea that the universe eventually *runs out of energy* is misleading. Complex process requires usable difference: temperature gradients, chemical-potential gradients, gravitational free energy, electrochemical gradients, and other departures from equilibrium. A heat engine requires reservoirs at different temperatures. A cell survives by maintaining chemical and electrical differences. A brain computes while actively preserving ionic gradients. Stars shine because matter has not already reached terminal equilibrium. What gets exhausted is the supply of exploitable differences rather than the energy itself.

That is not a new law of physics. It restates the point that organized macroscopic history depends on accessible free-energy gradients and that irreversible processes degrade them. (See: [Free-energy gradients](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#free-energy-gradients))

The theory therefore gains a physical spine:

> nonequilibrium initialization → differentiation → stars and chemistry → life → embodied history → dissipation → equilibrium tendency

The spine matters because it holds independently of the speculative metaphysics. Physics does not prove primordial agency. The model says only that if an antecedently integrated world is realized, thermodynamics supplies a real arrow along which it can become an irreversible history.

The core also does not require strict Laplacian micro-determinism. Contemporary quantum physics does not license the claim that science has demonstrated one classical trajectory fixed by exact microstate and law. What the theory needs is the weaker commitment of global historical determinacy: there is one complete actual history. Agents inside it still use probabilistic laws correctly to predict outcomes, and the underlying interpretation of quantum theory may be indeterministic. (See: [Global historical determinacy](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#global-historical-determinacy); [Research §3](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s3))

Determinacy is neither predictability nor fatalism. Chaotic systems can be unpredictable in practice, and a settled outcome still arrives through its causes. Medicine, deliberation, persuasion, institutions, and error stay causally real.

Once execution begins, freedom is no longer an endless supply of uncaused exceptions. The world becomes the temporal expression of antecedently integrated sourcehood, working through actual causes.

## The Celestial Clock {#the-celestial-clock}

Thermodynamics gives execution a direction. It does not give a fine-grained symbolic phase-coordinate for individual lives. The celestial extension asks whether the sky can do that second job.

The first layer is uncontroversial. At any moment the solar system has a calculable state: planetary positions, lunar phase, angular relations, eclipse geometry, rising and setting relations, and other quantities. Birth occurs at one such state, and later life occurs under later states. The sky is external to individual psychology, periodic or quasi-periodic, mathematically reconstructible, and historically used to organize calendars. Astronomy establishes the clock. (See: [Astronomical clock](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#astronomical-clock))

The second layer is historical and textual rather than physical. Genesis assigns celestial lights a role in marking signs, appointed times, days, and years, and Psalm 104 connects the moon with appointed times. Matthew's Magi interpret a celestial sign as historically significant. Mesopotamian omen traditions and later Hellenistic natal astrology developed increasingly elaborate grammars connecting celestial configurations with terrestrial conditions. Ptolemy explicitly nests the particular individual inside broader universal conditions. None of that demonstrates that the grammar is true. It shows that the architecture of shared sky, individual reference, and interpretation is historically real. (See: [Biblical calendrics](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#biblical-calendrics); [Magi](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#magi-and-the-star-of-bethlehem); [Enuma Anu Enlil](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#enuma-anu-enlil); [Genethlialogy](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#genethlialogy); [Ptolemaic nesting](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#ptolemaic-universal-particular-nesting))

The third layer is metaphysical. Plotinus is useful because he resists the claim that stars directly manufacture everything they signify. In an integrated cosmos, a visible celestial condition can belong to the same whole as a terrestrial condition without acting as a small efficient-causal beam. The model therefore says neither that *Saturn forces the event* nor that *the planet contains occult energy overriding physical law*. It asks whether the sky can be a readable projection of the same globally integrated history of which the person is another projection. (See: [Plotinian signification](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#plotinian-signification))

The fourth layer is the controversial one, where astrology proposes a semantics for the clock. Historical astrological rules would have to generate phase-descriptions restrictive enough to compare against independently defined classes of actual experience. Jungian archetypal language is useful only here, since it suggests how very different scenes can belong to one recurrent form: several lives can manifest severance, authority, creation, dependence, or transformation through nonidentical events. Jung does not prove astrology and does not supply the theory's parameter basis. His own failed statistical material is one reason the empirical burden has to stay explicit. (See: [Archetype](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#archetype); [Synchronicity](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#synchronicity); [Jung's marriage-horoscope test](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#jung-s-marriage-horoscope-test))

Thermodynamics supplies direction, astronomy supplies phase, astrology proposes semantics, and archetypal theory describes the class of manifestation.

The same sky therefore need not imply the same scene. People occupy different natal reference states, accumulated histories, social locations, bodies, and antecedent parameter structures. A common phase could in principle correspond to different events belonging to one abstract family. One person leaves a job, another a relationship, another a country, another a twenty-year project. The empirical question is not whether an interpreter can redescribe all four afterward as *closure*. It is whether a fixed phase-grammar, specified beforehand, can constrain the archetypal class of later events above appropriate null models.

That testability matters because the celestial layer is modular. If pre-registered astrological mappings fail prospectively, the phase-semantics extension has failed. Astronomy remains a clock, and no validated semantic decoder follows from it. Antecedent agency, providential integration, and the thermodynamic arrow would survive, because none of them logically depends on astrology.

Successful celestial-archetypal correlations would not prove premortal agency or Christianity either. They would strengthen one narrower claim: that the globally shared astronomical clock carries information about recurrent qualitative phases of terrestrial biography beyond its calendrical function.

Astrology is load-bearing only in that narrow sense. It does not make the engine run; it is the theory's proposed observable index of where the engine has reached in its execution. ([Research §4](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-research-program/#s4))

## The Physical Horizon {#the-physical-horizon}

A world whose organized processes depend on exploitable differences has a natural physical asymmetry. The same processes that make history possible also dissipate the gradients on which further organized history depends.

Over long enough timescales, stars exhaust usable fuel, stellar populations change, accessible gradients become sparse, and many standard cosmological scenarios approach dilute, low-activity conditions. Heat death belongs here, with a hierarchy of confidence attached. The Second Law and nonequilibrium thermodynamics are the hard anchor. The long-run decline of easily exploitable gradients is a strong physical expectation. A precisely specified final heat-death state is a conditional cosmological extrapolation, because the ultimate future depends on cosmological parameters, including the nature of dark energy. (See: [Heat death](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#heat-death); [Late-time cosmology](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#late-time-cosmology))

The theory does not need the exact final model to keep its physical spine. Its claim is that history is physically possible because the world has not already equilibrated, and that ordinary macroscopic process consumes the disequilibria sustaining organized change.

Buddhist cosmology offers a striking comparison that is not an identity. Buddhist texts and later cosmological systems imagine worlds contracting, dissolving, and forming again. That is not ancient thermodynamics. Its value is morphological: it thinks past the lifetime of one present world-system. Buddhist not-self doctrine meanwhile attacks the theory's strongest identity commitment. A system that wants one numerically continuous antecedent chooser cannot borrow Buddhist cosmology while ignoring anatta. (See: [Buddhist cosmic cycles](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#buddhist-cosmic-cycles); [Anatta](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#anatta-anatman))

Christian eschatology makes a different distinction possible. Resurrection, judgment, and new creation are not competing predictions about what ordinary matter will do after an enormous period of physical expansion. They are theological claims about divine consummation. One rule therefore survives every cosmological revision: a physical limit is not an eschatological resolution.

If an uninterrupted physical cosmos tends toward an equilibrium-like horizon, that describes what ordinary created processes approach under given assumptions. It does not decide whether God transforms creation, whether there is one cosmic run or more than one, or what becomes of persons past the natural physical limit. (See: [Christian eschaton](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#christian-eschaton); [One run or many](https://teocos.com/2026/08/fate-as-antecedent-freedom-references/#one-run-or-many))

The engine can run all the way to the limit physics describes without that limit carrying theology's final meaning.

## What Could Break It {#what-could-break-it}

A theory becomes more serious when it states how it can lose.

The core model is substantially weakened if any of the following failures prove unavoidable:

- no coherent relation can make the antecedent author and the embodied subject the same responsible person;
- no coherent account can distinguish an antecedent act that genuinely originates with the subject from either sufficient determination by a prior character or brute arbitrariness;
- every account of creaturely antecedence either eliminates genuine creaturely authorship or requires an incoherent mode of agency;
- the parameter basis has to be so vague that every life fits, or so concrete that it collapses into scene-selection;
- providential integration cannot distinguish authored constraints from unselected harms without becoming logically or morally arbitrary;
- the execution model requires claims that established physics contradicts, rather than commitments physics leaves underdetermined;

The celestial extension has its own failure condition. If a fixed astrological grammar cannot prospectively constrain independently classified archetypal phases above appropriate null models, or if every apparent success requires retrospective reinterpretation, astrology loses its proposed role as a semantic decoder. That failure would matter, and it would not show that antecedent sourcehood is impossible.

The remaining work is to pay the named debts rather than collect further parallels: identity, antecedence, authorship including the first-act problem, then integration, the parameter basis, and only afterward celestial testing. Those tasks live in the companion *Research Program*.

On this account fate is not the opposite of freedom. It is antecedent freedom after its possibilities have been made mutually compossible and handed over to irreversible history.
